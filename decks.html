Diagnostic Analysis 03 / 10
Systemic Bottlenecks

Three Root Causes, Not One

01

Psychiatrist Supply Deficit

Only 1 psychiatrist per 150,000 people — far below the WHO recommendation (3 per 100,000). Campus counselling cells serve thousands of students per counsellor, creating multi-week backlogs.

Structural Shortage
02

Identity Exposure Anxiety

Visiting a physical campus counselling cell is highly visible to peers, wardens, and faculty. The fear of being identified and stigmatized systematically outweighs the perceived pain of the mental distress itself.

Social Invalidation
03

Missing Stepped-Care Layer

India's mental health infrastructure is binary: passive generic articles vs. formal clinical therapy or emergency helplines like Tele-MANAS, with no low-friction middle tier for moderate distress.

Architectural Void

Any credible solution must be anonymous by design, scalable independent of psychiatrist supply, and stepped-care structured.

Stepped-Care Architecture

Smart Screening & Dynamic Triage

Mild Distress Tier 1

Self-Care

Guided breathing pacers, somatic grounding protocols (5-4-3-2-1), and CBT-based audio exercises for immediate low-intensity exam/placement stress.

Autonomous · No Human Dependent
Moderate Distress Tier 2

Peer Support

Socket.io-powered anonymous 1-on-1 peer-chat queue connecting student to PMHFA-trained peer volunteers for non-crisis isolation and venting.

Facilitated Empathy · High Scalability
Crisis / Severe Tier 3

Crisis Escalation

Immediate modal override bypassing screening. Direct 1-tap dial-out to Tele-MANAS (14416) and KIRAN (1800-599-0019) with dedicated campus SOS dispatch.
